## **Scoring Criteria (Total: 20 Points)**
### **1. Mean Squared Error (MSE) - 10 Points**
- Measures the average squared difference between predicted and actual scores.
- The formula for scoring:
  
  \[
  \text{Score} = \max\left(\min\left(\frac{(\text{Weak Baseline MSE} - \text{Your MSE}) \times 10}{(\text{Weak Baseline MSE} - \text{TA Baseline MSE})}, 10\right), 0\right)
  \]

- Your **private leaderboard MSE** determines your score.
- **Submission Limit:** **10 per day**.
